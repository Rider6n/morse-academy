Privacy Policy for Morse Academy

Effective date: 17 August 2026

Morse Academy is an Android learning application published by Rider6n. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Data collection and sharing

Morse Academy does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data with the developer or third parties.

The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, advertising identifiers, or other sensitive device permissions.

Learning progress and settings

The app stores learning progress and preferences locally on the user's device, such as completed lessons, quiz scores, mastered characters, streak information, sound settings, playback speed, theme preference, and onboarding status. These values are used only to provide the app's learning and personalization features.

This information is processed locally by the app and is not transmitted by Morse Academy to Rider6n or to third-party services.

Network services, advertising, and analytics

The current version of Morse Academy does not use advertising, analytics, user tracking, cloud accounts, or AI/network services during normal app operation. The app's learning content and Morse audio generation work offline.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored progress and preferences remain on the device until the user clears them. The app provides a Reset Learning Progress function in Settings. Users can also remove the app's local data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Because Rider6n does not receive or maintain user data on external servers, there is no server-side user profile or account data to delete.

Security

Morse Academy minimizes privacy risk by keeping its learning data on the user's device and by not transmitting personal or sensitive user data to the developer.

Children's privacy

Morse Academy does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. If the app's audience or data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety information will be updated accordingly.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if Morse Academy's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions about Morse Academy, please use the developer contact information published for Rider6n on the Morse Academy Google Play listing.
